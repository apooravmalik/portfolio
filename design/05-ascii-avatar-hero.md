# ASCII avatar hero — concept only

[Open the canvas](canvases/05-ascii-avatar-hero.png).

Proposed headline: **I turn ideas into things.** A small avatar sits beside “ideas”, with a bulb just above his head. Keep the orange ASCII donut on the right and the current portfolio palette and type family.

- Idle: curious face and unlit bulb.
- Hover or keyboard focus: eyes glance toward the pointer; bulb picks up orange.
- Tap or Enter: brief smile and orange bulb rays, then settle back.
- Touch: one tap triggers the same idea moment; hover is optional.
- Reduced motion: no tracking or travel; an instant expression/bulb state change.

Production intent: actual monospace ASCII glyphs on a fixed grid, no raster face or illustrated bulb. The generated canvas approximates glyph contours; exact expressions and letter positions will be designed in text when approved. Keep the avatar approximately 70–85px wide on desktop and 48–60px on phones, beside the headline without changing its line height or colliding with text. Give the avatar a keyboard-accessible control and a clear accessible name; never announce frames. The larger donut stays the main artwork.

The three lower storyboard columns are review material, not new portfolio content. The canvas is a static concept and does not implement the illustrated interactions. Built-in image generation used; prompts saved in prompts/05-ascii-avatar-hero.txt. Existing project design system supplied colours, typography and layout context. No new dependencies or live-site changes.

Status: approved and implemented locally on 20 October 2026. The canvas remains the design reference; the live hero uses real ASCII text and accessible controls.
